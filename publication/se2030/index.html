Foundation Model Engineering: Engineering Foundation Models Just as Engineering Software

Dezhi Ran, Mengzhou Wu, Wei Yang, Tao Xie

In TOSEM

Preprint

Summary

This vision paper treats foundation models as a form of software whose growing complexity calls for systematic engineering methods. It proposes foundation model engineering, emphasizing declarative, automated, and unified interfaces for data and model management. The goal is to make model development and application more structured while identifying new opportunities for software engineering research.
